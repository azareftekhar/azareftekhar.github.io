01 / About

Research and design grounded in human behavior.

My work brings psychology, digital culture, and user-centered research together to better understand the experiences people have with emerging technologies.

I am particularly interested in cyberpsychology, virtuality, digital identity, social connection, and the ethical design of AI-enabled systems. I also teach and develop learning experiences that help students critically engage with these rapidly changing areas.
