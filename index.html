About

About my work

My full name is Azar Eftekhar Nik, and I publish academically as Azar Eftekhar. I hold a PhD in Cyberpsychology and Social Psychology from the University of Wolverhampton and completed the Summer Doctoral Programme at the Oxford Internet Institute. My doctoral research examined personality as a predictor of visual self-presentation and motivations for photo-sharing on social media.

I have taught psychology and cyberpsychology in the United States and the United Kingdom, and I have also worked in industry as a UX researcher and training manager. These experiences inform my interest in the psychological, social, and ethical questions raised by everyday technology.

Researcher

I examine online behavior, identity, and the social and ethical implications of technology use.

Educator

Mentor

Research

Research interests

My research is grounded in psychology and uses qualitative, quantitative, and mixed-methods approaches. I use interviews, surveys, content analysis, and statistical analysis to investigate questions about human behavior in online and technology-mediated settings.

  • Online identity, self-presentation, and visual communication
  • Personality and social-media behavior
  • Trust, social presence, and anthropomorphism in AI
  • Algorithmic bias, privacy, and ethical technology design
  • Generative AI in teaching, learning, and research
  • Digital well-being, technostress, and online communities
