Azar Eftekhar Nik (publishing as Azar Eftekhar)

About Me

I hold a PhD in Psychology with a specialization in cyberpsychology and a master’s degree in Information Science. With more than 16 years of teaching and research experience, my work examines the intersection of psychology, user behavior, and digital and emerging technologies.

I have designed and taught a wide range of undergraduate and graduate courses in-person and online at institutions including the University of South Carolina and Norfolk State University. My teaching includes Foundations of Psychology, Personality, Cognition, Cyberpsychology, Research Methods and Ethics, and Data Visualization. I have developed a strong record of student engagement and student satisfaction, supported by consistently positive student and peer evaluations. I am also a certified online instructor.

My research focuses on personality, online identity, user behavior, and human-centered AI. At Norfolk State University, I serve as a research committee chair and mentor master’s students in the M.S. in Cyberpsychology program. I am also an affiliated member of CRUW (Cyberpsychology Research at the University of Wolverhampton). Learn more about my scholarly work by exploring my selected publications, and feel free to connect with me.

Research

Research Interests

My research is grounded in psychology and uses qualitative, quantitative, and mixed-methods approaches. I use interviews, surveys, content analysis, and statistical analysis to investigate questions about human behavior in online and technology-mediated settings.

  • Online identity, self-presentation, and visual communication
  • Personality and social-media behavior
  • Trust, social presence, and anthropomorphism in AI
  • Algorithmic bias, privacy, and ethical technology design
  • Generative AI in teaching, learning, and research
  • Digital well-being, technostress, and online communities
